Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

M ONE Logistics ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use the M ONE Logistics application.

Depending on how you use the application, we may process:

  • Phone number and account information;
  • User profile information;
  • Shipment and logistics information;
  • Client and business-related information entered by users;
  • Payment, debt, and transaction-related information entered in the application;
  • Messages or support requests sent through the application;
  • Technical information required for the operation and security of the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • create and manage user accounts;
  • provide logistics and shipment management services;
  • display shipment, client, debt, and payment information to authorized users;
  • provide customer support;
  • maintain application security;
  • improve the reliability and functionality of the application.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as hosting, database, authentication, or infrastructure providers.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

6. Children's Privacy

M ONE Logistics is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the service.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the M ONE Logistics support team through the contact information provided in the application.
